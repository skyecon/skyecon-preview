What matters on a Delta flooring project

  • Detached homes and townhouses where existing flooring removal, subfloor repair, height changes, stairs, and baseboards can be as important as laying the new material.
  • Concrete or moisture-sensitive conditions where testing, product compatibility, underlay, primer, patching, or levelling may be required.
  • LVP, laminate, and engineered hardwood installations where room connections, expansion, door clearances, and tile transitions need advance planning.
  • Occupied-home projects where careful staging, floor protection, direct communication, and clean daily work reduce disruption.
