What matters on a Vancouver flooring project

  • Condo and strata projects where access, elevator bookings, working hours, sound-control requirements, and tidy common-area protection need to be planned before installation.
  • Concrete slabs that need flatness checks, moisture-aware preparation, patching, levelling, and adhesive compatibility reviewed before glue-down flooring begins.
  • Premium engineered hardwood, wide plank, herringbone, stairs, and tight-to-tile transitions where layout and visible finish quality justify a higher-control installation.
  • Occupied homes and renovations where dust control, clean sequencing, direct communication, and coordination with cabinets, tile, doors, and baseboards matter.
