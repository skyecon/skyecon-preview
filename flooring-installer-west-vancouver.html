What matters on a West Vancouver flooring project

  • Wide plank, engineered hardwood, herringbone, and double glue-down systems where product instructions, layout, adhesive, open time, rolling pressure, and sequencing must be followed.
  • Large rooms and long sightlines where flatness, board direction, starting lines, feature areas, and expansion planning directly affect the visual result.

  • Occupied-home renovations where floor protection, dust control, daily cleanup, careful material handling, and coordination with designers or other trades matter.
