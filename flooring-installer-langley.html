What matters on a Langley flooring project

  • Large connected areas where layout lines, board direction, expansion, product batch planning, and room-to-room flow need to be established before installation.
  • Townhomes and multi-storey houses where stairs, landings, nosings, transitions, and baseboards need consistent finish details.
  • Basement or concrete areas where moisture, flatness, patching, levelling, underlay, and product suitability should be resolved first.
  • Newer and renovated homes where cabinets, islands, fireplaces, tile, doors, and existing trim affect sequencing and transition design.
