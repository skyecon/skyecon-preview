What matters on a Burnaby flooring project

  • High-rise and strata flooring work where approved underlay, acoustic requirements, elevator access, protection, and work-hour rules need to be confirmed before material goes down.
  • Concrete slab preparation for glue-down LVP or engineered hardwood, including flatness, moisture, patching, primer, levelling, adhesive spread, and rolling requirements.
  • Older homes where additions, previous renovations, squeaks, height changes, and uneven transitions can require corrective prep rather than covering problems.
  • Stairs, nosings, baseboards, and tight-to-tile transitions that make the completed floor look integrated with the rest of the renovation.
