Hardwood Flooring Penticton

Hardwood floors are unforgiving. The slab, layout, adhesive, expansion, and baseboard details all have to be right before the room ever looks premium.

Pricing: Engineered hardwood glue-down from $4.00/sq ft; wide plank and herringbone patterns quoted higher.

Skyecon focuses on engineered hardwood, glue-down, floating, wide plank, and pattern work — not nail-down solid hardwood.

Problems this solves

  • Customers comparing engineered hardwood, wide plank, patterns, or premium flooring but unsure what the room can actually support.
  • Concrete or wood subfloors that need moisture checks, flatness checks, patching, or better prep before the floor goes down.
  • Layouts where plank direction, board width, doorways, transitions, baseboards, and finish details decide whether the finished floor feels premium.
  • Material already purchased but no installer you trust to handle glue-down, floating, stairs, transitions, baseboards, and finish details cleanly.

Process

  • Review product specs, room photos, square footage, subfloor type, and timeline.
  • Check moisture risk, flatness, expansion needs, layout direction, doorways, transitions, stairs, and baseboard details.
  • Quote the labour clearly before work starts, including prep or pattern complexity where needed.
  • Install with prep-first sequencing, clean cuts, careful adhesive/control where required, and a finished detail pass.
