What matters on a New Westminster flooring project

  • Older homes where squeaks, patchwork subfloors, additions, height changes, and out-of-flat areas may need repair or levelling before new flooring is installed.
  • Condos and strata buildings where acoustic assemblies, concrete preparation, access rules, and common-area protection should be confirmed early.
  • Engineered hardwood, LVP, and laminate installations where floating versus glue-down decisions depend on the product, substrate, room, and finish expectations.
  • Stairs, baseboards, casing, and transitions where accurate fitting helps new flooring belong in an existing interior rather than looking added afterward.
