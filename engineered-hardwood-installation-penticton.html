Engineered Hardwood Installation Penticton

Engineered hardwood is a strong choice for Okanagan homes when moisture, concrete, product requirements, and pattern layout are handled properly — including herringbone where the room and material suit it.

Pricing: Engineered glue-down from $4.00–$6.00/sq ft; double glue-down from $5.50–$8.00/sq ft.

Problems this solves

  • Concrete slabs or older subfloors where moisture readings, patching, primer, and adhesive compatibility matter.
  • Premium products that need acclimation, correct trowel notch, open time, pressure, and sequencing — not guesswork.
  • Wide plank, glue-down, floating, double glue-down, or herringbone layouts where small layout errors become expensive.
  • Homeowners who already bought engineered hardwood and need a product-aware installer before boxes get opened.

Process

  • Review the product installation guide, photos, slab/subfloor, room dimensions, and timeline.
  • Check moisture, flatness, adhesive requirements, plank width, layout direction, transitions, and herringbone sequencing if applicable.
  • Confirm prep scope, labour price, and risk areas before installation starts.
  • Install according to product requirements with clean layout, controlled adhesive work, careful cuts, and baseboard-ready finishing.
