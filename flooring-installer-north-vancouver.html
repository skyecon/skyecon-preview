What matters on a North Vancouver flooring project

  • Strata and condo installations where underlay approvals, acoustic performance, access, common-area protection, and renovation schedules should be resolved before work starts.
  • Multi-level homes and townhouses where stair treads, risers, nosings, landings, and transitions require careful sequencing and accurate fitting.
  • Entry areas and active households where durable LVP, engineered flooring, clean thresholds, and product-appropriate moisture management matter.
  • Renovations where subfloor flatness, existing tile heights, doors, fireplaces, cabinetry, and baseboards affect the final flooring layout.
