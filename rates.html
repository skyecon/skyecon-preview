Flooring Installation Pricing

All rates below are labour only and flooring-focused. You supply the materials — I don't mind where they come from, just have them on site. I'm happy to advise on quantities, product requirements, and install method if you want help. Prices are starting ranges for normal conditions; final quotes depend on the material, subfloor, prep, layout, stairs, transitions, access, travel, and schedule.

Local and practical service areas include the Lower Mainland, the Okanagan, and Bowen Island. Select BC-wide work is considered when the scope, material, schedule, and finish standard justify travel; accommodation and travel are quoted separately when needed.

Not sure what level you need? Most rentals and basic updates fit Good when the subfloor is ready. Glue-down products, concrete slabs, and premium material usually belong in Better. Double glue-down, wide plank, herringbone, and high-control layouts belong in Best.

Good

Floating laminate / LVP

Clean, cost-effective flooring when the subfloor is ready. Still needs flatness, underlay, expansion, clean cuts, and proper transitions.

Better

Glue-down LVP / engineered

More controlled installation with adhesive compatibility, spread, open time, rolling pressure, substrate prep, and finished detail handled properly.

Best

Double glue-down / wide plank / herringbone

Premium method for projects where layout, sequencing, flatness, sound/comfort expectations, and visible finish quality matter more.

Hardwood Installation

Glue‑down and floating only — no nail‑down. Floating can be appropriate when the product and subfloor suit it. Glue‑down gives stronger control and a more stable feel, especially on concrete and premium installations. Double glue‑down is the premium method: glue the underlay to the subfloor, then glue the flooring on top. Wide plank, herringbone, and complex layouts require more prep, sequencing, and layout discipline.

Engineered hardwood (glue‑down) —$4.00 – $6.00/sq ft
Hardwood (floating, click) —$3.00 – $4.50/sq ft

Herringbone / chevron —$6.00 – $10.00/sq ft
Wide‑plank (8"+) —$5.00 – $7.50/sq ft
